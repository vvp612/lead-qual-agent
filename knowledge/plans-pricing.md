# Plans & pricing

ShopDesk has three plans billed per workspace, monthly or annual (two months free).

- **Starter** — small teams, core inventory and order tracking.
- **Growth** — adds automations, API access and multi-location.
- **Enterprise** — SSO, audit log export, dedicated support and an SLA.

Discounts: registered non-profits and education get 30%; annual billing saves two months.

## Sales signals to treat as a hot lead

A message is high-value when it mentions **pricing, a quote, bulk/volume, an enterprise
plan, SSO, an API integration, a demo, or a partnership**. Route these to sales and offer
a call. Enterprise and integration mentions are the strongest buying signals.
