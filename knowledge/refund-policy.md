# Refund & return policy

ShopDesk offers a 30-day money-back guarantee on all subscriptions, no questions asked.

- Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 business days.
- Annual plans refunded after month 1 are credited pro-rata minus two months.
- To request a refund, the customer replies "refund" or contacts billing@shopdesk.example.
- Chargebacks without a prior refund request are escalated to the account team.

If a customer is angry about a refund delay, apologise and escalate — a human responds
the same business day.
