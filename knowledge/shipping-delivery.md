# Shipping & delivery

Standard delivery is 3–5 business days within the region; express is next-day for a fee.

- Order tracking emails are sent automatically at dispatch; a missing tracking email
  usually means the order is still in the picking queue, not lost.
- International orders ship DDP; customs fees are covered by ShopDesk above $150.
- Damaged-on-arrival: customers send one photo and a replacement ships immediately —
  this is a same-day, human-handled path.

Escalate any shipping complaint that mentions a lawyer, attorney or chargeback.
