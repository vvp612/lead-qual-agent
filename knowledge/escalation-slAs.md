# Escalation & support SLAs

The triage agent should handle routine questions itself and escalate the rest.

Escalate to a human when the message:
- is a complaint, or mentions a refund delay, attorney, chargeback or a serious outage;
- concerns billing on an annual or enterprise plan;
- a paying customer reports a **data loss, security or account-access** problem.

Never invent a policy that is not in a source document. When unsure, say a teammate will
follow up rather than guessing. Support SLA: hot leads and escalations get a first
response within one business hour during 09:00–18:00 local time.
